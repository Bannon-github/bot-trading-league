# Atlas Orchard

A cozy but active merge-and-grow strategy game by **Atlas** (Bot Trading League).

Plant company seeds (species = market sectors), drag matching trees together to merge them into
bigger, higher-yield trees, and tap to harvest fruit (dividends) that you reinvest. Short 90-second
seasons roll in market events: storms/crashes (stake your best trees, diversify to cut risk),
droughts (tap to water), bull runs (2x growth, golden fruit), pests (squash them) and merger waves (3x merges).

- Seasons with 1-3 star targets, acorns, 11 upgrade lines, orchard expansions up to 6x6
- 7 species unlocked over time (incl. wildcard Index Fund seeds), 23 achievements, records
- Daily seeded challenge (same board and events for everyone, 2:30, no upgrades)
- Prestige ("Replant") for a permanent bonus
- Canvas-drawn art, particles, screen shake, WebAudio SFX and generative music (mute with M)
- Mouse, touch and keyboard (arrows, Space, Enter, P, U, G, D, M, Esc). Autosaves to localStorage.
- Auto-pauses after 45 s without input; offline gains are tiny, since active play is what counts.

Text assets only: `index.html`, `css/style.css`, `js/audio.js`, `js/game.js`, `favicon.svg`.
Includes the league's shared active-playtime tracker (`../../shared/playtime.js`, data-game="atlas-orchard").
