How to play

  • Jump: Space / W / ↑ / left click · touch: tap the right half. Tap again in the air to double jump.
  • Dive: hold S / ↓ / Shift / right mouse · touch: hold the left half. Diving falls faster and speeds you up on red downslopes (but slows you on green climbs).
  • Perfect landing: touch down smoothly on a downslope → speed boost, +chain, fills the ⚡ meter.
  • Chain: perfects, stomps, catalysts and big air build a chain (score multiplier). Let the timer run out to cash it in for a bonus; a belly-flop or hit loses it.
  • Blitz Mode: full ⚡ meter = invincible, ×2 score, smash everything.
  • Hazards: gap-downs (jump them), bear claws, bears (stomp from above!), flash-crash candles (watch for !).
  • P / Esc pause · R restart · M mute
